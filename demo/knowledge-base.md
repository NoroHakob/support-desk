# Brightline help center

Brightline makes the Brightline desk lamp and the Brightline app that controls it.

## Accounts

### How do I reset my password?

Open the Brightline app and tap **Sign in**, then **Forgot password**. Enter the email you signed up with and we'll send you a link. The link works for one hour. If the email doesn't arrive within five minutes, check your spam folder or ask us to send it again.

### Can I change my email address?

Yes. In the app, go to **Settings → Account → Email**, enter the new address and confirm it from the email we send.

## Plans and pricing

### What plans are there?

- **Free**: $0. The daylight schedule and three focus scenes, for one lamp.
- **Pro**: $4 per month, or $40 per year. Up to 10 lamps, unlimited scenes, and sleep and wake routines.

The lamp itself costs $129 as a one-time purchase. You don't need a plan to use it.

### How do I cancel Pro?

Go to **Settings → Plan → Cancel Pro** in the app. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for. Your lamps go back to the Free features after that.

## Orders, shipping and returns

### Where do you ship?

We ship to the United States, Canada and the European Union. Shipping is free on every order. Orders arrive in 3–5 business days in the United States, 5–8 in Canada, and 5–10 in the European Union.

### What's your refund policy?

You can return the lamp within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. It must come back with everything that was in the box. We email you a free return label. Refunds reach your original payment method within 5 business days after we receive the lamp.

Pro subscriptions aren't refunded for the current period, but you can cancel at any time.

### What does the warranty cover?

The lamp has a two-year warranty against defects. It doesn't cover damage from drops or water.

## Troubleshooting

### The lamp won't connect to the app

1. Make sure your phone's Bluetooth is on.
2. Hold the button under the lamp's base for 10 seconds until the light blinks twice.
3. In the app, tap **Add lamp** and follow the steps.

The lamp needs a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. 5 GHz-only networks aren't supported.

### The light is flickering

Unplug the lamp for 30 seconds and plug it back in. If it still flickers, contact us and we'll replace it under warranty.
